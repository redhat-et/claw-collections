# Campaign Brief Generator

Generate a structured campaign brief to align stakeholders
before execution.

## Required inputs

Ask the user for:
- **Campaign name** and **product or initiative**
- **Objective**: What business outcome should this drive?
- **Target audience**: Who are we trying to reach?
- **Timeline**: Start and end dates
- **Budget range** (if known)

## Brief structure

### 1. Executive summary

2-3 sentences: what we are doing, why, and expected impact.

### 2. Objectives and KPIs

| Objective | KPI | Target | Measurement Method |
|-----------|-----|--------|--------------------|

Use SMART criteria. Limit to 3-5 objectives.

### 3. Target audience

- Primary persona with demographics, pain points, and
  motivations
- Secondary persona (if applicable)
- Exclusions: who is explicitly NOT the target

### 4. Messaging framework

- **Value proposition**: one sentence
- **Key messages**: 3 supporting points
- **Proof points**: data, testimonials, or case studies
  that back each message

### 5. Channel strategy

| Channel | Role | Content Type | Cadence |
|---------|------|-------------|---------|

### 6. Timeline

Milestone table with dates, deliverables, and owners.

### 7. Budget allocation

Breakdown by channel or tactic. Flag any line items that
need approval.

### 8. Risks and dependencies

2-3 items that could delay or derail the campaign, with
mitigation actions.

## Output format

Use markdown with clear section headers. Keep the entire
brief under 800 words.
