# Content Calendar Builder

Build a structured content calendar to coordinate publishing
across channels.

## Required inputs

Ask the user for:
- **Planning period** (e.g., July 2026, Q3 2026)
- **Channels**: which platforms to include (blog, email,
  LinkedIn, X/Twitter, etc.)
- **Themes or campaigns** to align content around
- **Publishing cadence** per channel (e.g., blog 2x/week,
  email 1x/week)
- **Team members** responsible for content (for the Owner
  column)

## Calendar structure

### Overview

Summarize the period's content strategy in 2-3 sentences:
themes, goals, and any key dates (product launches, events,
holidays) that anchor the calendar.

### Content calendar table

| Week | Date | Channel | Content Type | Topic | CTA | Owner | Status |
|------|------|---------|-------------|-------|-----|-------|--------|

Pre-fill Status as "Planned" for all items.

### Content types to consider

- Blog posts (thought leadership, how-to, product updates)
- Email newsletters and nurture sequences
- Social media posts (organic and paid)
- Webinars or live events
- Case studies and customer stories
- Infographics and visual assets

### Coordination notes

- Flag content that depends on other teams (product, sales,
  design) and note the dependency
- Mark repurposing opportunities (e.g., blog post becomes
  email becomes social thread)
- Note any approval gates (legal review, exec sign-off)

## Output format

Use a markdown table. For periods longer than one month,
break into monthly sections. Include a summary row at the
bottom with total pieces per channel.
